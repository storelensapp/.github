# Ecommerce competitor research by Storelens

Ecommerce competitor research for any online store: store spy reports, a theme detector and app detector, competitor product tracking, price tracking, best sellers and store leads. See what any store is built with, what it sells and who runs it.

- [storelensapp.com](https://storelensapp.com): theme detector and app detector for Shopify stores (free account, no card), store spy, competitor product and price tracking, Shopify store leads
- MCP server: `https://storelensapp.com/mcp` ([docs](https://storelensapp.com/developers/))
- [mcp-server](https://github.com/storelensapp/mcp-server) · [plugin](https://github.com/storelensapp/plugin) · [agent-skill](https://github.com/storelensapp/agent-skill)
